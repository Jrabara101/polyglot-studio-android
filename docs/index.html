Five games, each one trains a different skill

Rounds take a couple of minutes. Every round earns XP toward your streak and your mastery in that skill.

  • LexiMatch Cards

    Vocabulary

    Pair scripts with their meanings before your combo streak runs out. The round above is a small version.

  • Ink & Stroke Studio

    Writing

    Draw characters on a canvas with your finger. Gemini Vision checks your strokes and tells you what to fix.

  • Syntax Sprint Quiz

    Grammar

    Quick rounds on particles, word order and sentence building.

  • Echo Voice Arena

    Pronunciation

    Hear a native-voiced prompt, say it back, and get feedback on how close you were.

  • Story Café

    Conversation

    Order coffee in Paris or tapas in Madrid. Pick your replies and the story follows them.

Three languages, taught in context

Each language has its own stories, phrases and pronunciation guides, written for English speakers.

Practise speaking with a live AI tutor

The Gemini Live tutor listens as you speak and coaches you in real time, so you can practise out loud whenever you like.

  • Speech coaching on your pronunciation as you talk
  • XP, daily streaks and skill mastery, synced to your account
  • Save phrases you want to keep and review them later

Install in three steps

Polyglot Studio is installed from an APK file while it’s in early access.

  1. Download the APK

    On your Android phone, open the latest release and tap the .apk file.

  2. Allow the install

    If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. You can turn this off again afterwards.

  3. Open and start playing

    Sign in with Google to sync your progress, or continue as a guest.
